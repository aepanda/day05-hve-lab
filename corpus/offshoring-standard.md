# Vendor Offshoring and Data Location Standard

Doc ID: OFFS-STD
Version: 1.0
Owner: TPRM Office
Effective: 2026-11-01
Status: Current

## 1. Purpose

This standard sets the conditions under which a vendor may perform services for Northwind Capital Group, or store or access Northwind data, from a location outside the country in which the contracting Northwind entity is established ("offshoring").

## 2. Scope

The standard applies to every Tier 1 and Tier 2 vendor (see TIER-MTX) and to any Tier 3 vendor that processes personal data. It covers the vendor's own staff and facilities and those of its material subcontractors (see FRTH-POL).

## 3. Disclosure

3.1 Every in-scope vendor must disclose, at onboarding and at each reassessment under DUE-STD, each country from which it will perform services, store Northwind data, or access Northwind systems.

3.2 A change to the disclosed locations is a material change that triggers an early reassessment under TIER-MTX §3. The vendor must give at least 90 days' written notice of a new location for Tier 1 services and 60 days for Tier 2.

## 4. Approval

4.1 Offshoring of a Tier 1 service requires approval from the Chief Risk Officer, supported by a location risk assessment completed by the TPRM Office.

4.2 Offshoring of a Tier 2 service, or of personal-data processing by a Tier 3 vendor, requires approval from the Head of TPRM.

4.3 Storage of Restricted data outside the contracting entity's country is prohibited unless the CISO approves an exception under EXC-PRC with compensating controls, including customer-managed encryption keys held by Northwind.

## 5. Location Risk Assessment

The location risk assessment considers, at minimum: the legal regime for government access to data; sanctions exposure under SANC-PRC; political and infrastructure stability; the availability of the right to audit on site (AUDT-GDE); and the effect on business continuity targets under BCP-REQ.

## 6. Contract Requirements

Contracts for offshored services must name the approved locations, prohibit new locations without Northwind's prior written approval, and require the vendor to flow these terms down to subcontractors. Data processing terms follow DPA-REQ.

## 7. Monitoring

The Vendor Manager confirms the vendor's service locations at each quarterly business review for Tier 1 vendors and at each semi-annual review for Tier 2 vendors under PERF-STD. An undisclosed location discovered in monitoring is reported to the TPRM Office within 5 business days and treated as a High finding.
