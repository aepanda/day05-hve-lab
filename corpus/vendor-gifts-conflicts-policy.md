# Gifts, Entertainment and Vendor Conflicts of Interest Policy

Doc ID: GIFT-POL
Version: 3.3
Owner: Chief Compliance Officer
Effective: September 1, 2025
Status: Current

**Purpose.** Vendor decisions at Northwind must be made on merit. Gifts, hospitality, and personal relationships can influence those decisions, or appear to. This policy sets the limits for employees who select, manage, or approve payments to vendors, and for any employee who receives gifts or entertainment from a current or prospective vendor.

**Scope.** All Northwind employees and contractors. The policy covers gifts and entertainment offered by any current vendor, any prospective vendor, and any vendor's agent or subcontractor.

**Gifts.** An employee may accept a gift from a vendor only if its value is $100 or less. The aggregate value of gifts accepted from any one vendor may not exceed $250 per calendar year per employee. Gifts above these limits must be politely declined or, if refusal would cause offense, surrendered to Compliance within 5 business days. Cash and cash equivalents, including gift cards, prepaid cards, vouchers, and securities, are prohibited at any value.

**Entertainment.** Business meals and events attended together with the vendor are entertainment, not gifts. Entertainment of up to $250 per person per event may be accepted without pre-approval if it has a clear business purpose and is not lavish or frequent. Entertainment above $250 per person requires pre-approval from the employee's manager and Compliance. Entertainment where the vendor does not attend is treated as a gift. Travel and accommodation paid by a vendor are prohibited unless pre-approved by Compliance.

**Blackout during sourcing.** Employees involved in an active RFP or sole-source evaluation (PRC-POL) may not accept any gift or entertainment, of any value, from any bidder until the contract is awarded. The blackout also applies during contract renewal negotiations (RNW-PRC).

**Register.** Every gift or entertainment offered by a vendor and valued above $50, whether accepted or declined, must be recorded in the Gifts and Entertainment Register within 5 business days. Register entries are retained under RET-SCH.

**Giving to vendors.** Gifts and entertainment given by Northwind employees to vendor personnel follow the same value limits and must be recorded in the register.

**Conflicts of interest.** A conflict exists where an employee, or a close family member or household member, has a financial interest in a vendor, is employed by a vendor, or has a close personal relationship with a vendor's owner or account team. Employees must disclose a conflict to Compliance as soon as they become aware of it and before taking part in any decision involving that vendor.

**Recusal.** An employee with a disclosed conflict must recuse from vendor selection, evaluation, contract negotiation, performance rating, invoice approval, and exception approval for that vendor. RFP evaluators sign a conflict declaration before reviewing bids (PRC-POL §7.2).

**Annual attestation.** All employees with vendor-facing responsibilities, including every Vendor Manager and Business Owner, complete an annual conflicts-of-interest attestation by January 31 each year.

**Former employees.** A Northwind employee who leaves to join a vendor may not represent that vendor to Northwind for 12 months on any matter they handled while employed at Northwind.

**Breaches.** Failure to disclose a conflict or to record a gift is a disciplinary matter. Where a breach affected a sourcing decision, Compliance may require the sourcing to be re-run.
