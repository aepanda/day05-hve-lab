# Vendor Financial Health Review Standard

Doc ID: FIN-STD
Version: 1.5
Owner: Treasury Risk — Counterparty Analysis
Effective: 2026-02-15
Status: Current

## 1. Purpose

A vendor that cannot pay its own bills cannot reliably deliver services to Northwind. This standard defines when and how vendor financial health is reviewed and what Northwind does when a vendor's position deteriorates.

## 2. Scope and Frequency

Financial review is required for Tier 1 and Tier 2 vendors:

- at onboarding, as part of due diligence (DUE-STD §4.4);
- annually for Tier 1 vendors;
- every 24 months for Tier 2 vendors;
- whenever a trigger event in Section 5 occurs.

Tier 3 and Tier 4 vendors are not reviewed unless the TPRM Office requests it.

## 3. Evidence

3.1 Public companies: the last two annual reports and the most recent quarterly filing.

3.2 Private companies: audited financial statements for the last two fiscal years.

3.3 Where a private vendor cannot provide audited statements, Treasury Risk may accept, in order of preference: reviewed statements signed by an independent accountant; a third-party credit report; or management accounts with a signed officer certification. Management accounts alone are not sufficient for a Tier 1 vendor.

## 4. Assessment

Treasury Risk assigns a rating of Green, Amber, or Red. The following indicators each move the rating toward Amber, and two or more toward Red:

- current ratio below 1.0;
- negative operating cash flow in each of the last two fiscal years;
- revenue decline of more than 20% year on year;
- an external credit rating below BB-, or an equivalent third-party risk score in the bottom quartile;
- a going-concern qualification in the auditor's opinion (this alone produces Red);
- Northwind spend greater than 25% of the vendor's annual revenue, indicating the vendor depends on Northwind.

## 5. Trigger Events

The following require a review within 15 business days of Northwind becoming aware: a change of control; a credit rating downgrade of two or more notches; a missed debt payment or covenant breach; announced layoffs of more than 15% of staff; loss of a major client publicly reported; or a going-concern warning. Vendors must notify these events under INCN-SLA.

## 6. Watch List

6.1 Vendors rated Amber or Red are placed on the financial watch list. Treasury Risk reviews the watch list quarterly and reports it to the TPRC.

6.2 For an Amber rating, the Vendor Manager confirms the exit plan (EXIT-PLN) is current and increases monitoring of service performance.

6.3 For a Red rating, the TPRM Office assesses invoking the stressed exit plan under EXIT-PLN §5, and the Business Owner must not commit to any new spend or contract extension until the TPRC has reviewed the vendor.

## 7. Prepayments

Prepayments of more than 3 months' fees to any vendor on the watch list are prohibited. For other Tier 1 and Tier 2 vendors, prepayments of more than 6 months' fees require Treasury Risk approval.

## 8. Records

Financial review files form part of the due diligence file and are retained under RET-SCH.
