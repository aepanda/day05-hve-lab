# Vendor Onboarding Procedure

Doc ID: ONB-PRC
Version: 2.4
Owner: Procurement Operations Lead
Effective: 03/15/2026
Status: Current

## Overview

This procedure takes a vendor from approved sourcing decision to an active record in the vendor master. It applies after Procurement has completed sourcing under PRC-POL. No vendor may receive Northwind data, system access, or payment until Step 7 is complete (TPR-POL §4.3).

## Step 1: Intake

The Business Owner submits a Vendor Intake Request in ProcureNet. The request must state the business need, estimated annual spend, the data classifications the vendor will access, the business service supported, and whether the vendor will use subcontractors. Incomplete requests are returned within 2 business days.

## Step 2: Tiering

The TPRM Office assigns a preliminary tier under TIER-MTX within 5 business days of a complete intake request. The tier and the list of required assessments are posted to the ProcureNet record.

## Step 3: Screening

Procurement initiates sanctions and adverse media screening under SANC-PRC. Screening must return clear, or any potential match must be adjudicated, before diligence evidence is requested from the vendor. This prevents Northwind from sharing its questionnaires with a sanctioned party.

## Step 4: Due Diligence

The TPRM Office coordinates the assessments required by DUE-STD. The vendor receives a single consolidated evidence request. Vendors have 15 business days to return evidence; one extension of 10 business days may be granted by the TPRM Office.

## Step 5: Contracting

Legal negotiates the contract using the clause library for the vendor's tier. Where personal data is involved, a DPA meeting DPA-REQ must be executed with or before the main agreement. Tier 1 and Tier 2 contracts must include an exit plan schedule (EXIT-PLN) and a right-to-audit clause (AUDT-GDE).

## Step 6: Banking and Tax Verification

Accounts Payable verifies the vendor's tax identification and bank details. Bank details must be confirmed by a callback to a telephone number obtained independently of the vendor's submission, never a number on the submitted form. Any later change to bank details requires the same callback before the change is applied.

## Step 7: Activation

Procurement creates the vendor master record only after the TPRM Office records final approval in ProcureNet. The Vendor Manager is named on the record at activation. Access requests for vendor personnel may then be raised under ACC-STD.

## Cycle-Time Targets

| Tier | Target from complete intake to activation |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 45 business days |
| Tier 2 | 30 business days |
| Tier 3 | 15 business days |
| Tier 4 | 5 business days |

Targets exclude time the vendor spends returning evidence beyond the 15-business-day window.

## Common Errors

- Raising a purchase order before activation. ProcureNet blocks this, and manual workarounds are a policy breach.
- Sharing Confidential data during a pilot before onboarding. Pilots are onboarding; tier them the same way.
- Treating a renewal as a new vendor. Renewals follow RNW-PRC.
