## 1 Purpose

This standard describes the due diligence the Vendor Risk Management Team performs before Northwind contracts with a supplier and at periodic review. It supports the Third-Party Risk Management Policy.

## 2 Scope

All suppliers with an annual contract value above $25,000. Suppliers below this value are exempt from due diligence.

## 3 Timing

Due diligence should be completed before contract signature where practicable. Where business urgency requires, the contract may be signed with diligence completed within 30 days afterwards, subject to Business Owner sign-off.

Diligence results are valid for 180 days from approval.

## 4 Assessment Evidence

### 4.1 Questionnaire

Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers complete the Standard Vendor Questionnaire. Tier 3 and Tier 4 suppliers are not required to complete a questionnaire.

### 4.2 Assurance Reports

Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers provide a SOC 2 report. Either a SOC 2 Type I or a SOC 2 Type II report is accepted. The report must be dated no more than 18 months before the review date. An ISO/IEC 27001 certificate may substitute for any tier without further approval.

### 4.3 Penetration Testing

Penetration test results are requested for Tier 1 suppliers where available. Absence of a test is not a finding.

### 4.4 Financial Review

Financial statements are reviewed for Tier 1 suppliers only.

## 5 Periodic Review

| Tier | Review frequency |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | Every 24 months |
| Tier 2 | Every 36 months |
| Tier 3 | At renewal only |
| Tier 4 | Not reviewed |

The relationship owner starts the review 30 days before the due date.

## 6 Findings

High findings must have a remediation plan agreed before contract signature; remediation itself may complete after onboarding. Medium findings must be remediated within 12 months. Low findings are not tracked.

## 7 Sanctions

Suppliers are screened against sanctions lists at onboarding and annually thereafter.

## 8 Records

Due diligence files are retained for 5 years after the review date.
