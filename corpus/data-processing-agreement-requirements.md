# Data Processing Agreement Requirements

Doc ID: DPA-REQ
Version: 2.2
Owner: Legal — Privacy Counsel
Effective: 15 January 2026
Status: Current

## 1. When a DPA Is Required

A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is required before any personal data is shared with a vendor, in any volume, including test or pilot data. "Personal data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, including client, employee, and prospect data. Pseudonymized data is still personal data. Fully anonymized aggregate data is not, but the Privacy Office must confirm the anonymization method in writing.

A missing DPA cannot be covered by an exception under EXC-PRC. If personal data is involved, the DPA is mandatory.

## 2. Mandatory Clauses

Legal uses the Northwind DPA template. Vendor paper is accepted only if it contains every clause below:

1. Processing only on Northwind's documented instructions, with the subject matter, duration, nature, purpose, data categories, and data subject categories listed in an annex.
2. Confidentiality commitments binding every person authorized to process the data.
3. Security measures at least equivalent to those evidenced during due diligence (DUE-STD §4).
4. Sub-processor controls: no new sub-processor without 30 days' prior written notice to Northwind and a right to object. Sub-processors are fourth parties and are also governed by FRTH-POL.
5. Assistance with data subject rights requests, with a response to Northwind within 5 business days of a request.
6. Breach notification within the deadlines in INCN-SLA, and in any case no later than 72 hours after the vendor becomes aware of a personal-data breach.
7. Assistance with data protection impact assessments and regulator consultations.
8. Deletion or return of all personal data at the end of the services, with certification, as set out in EXIT-PLN §4.2.
9. Audit and inspection rights consistent with AUDT-GDE.
10. A prohibition on using Northwind personal data to train, fine-tune, or improve the vendor's products or models without Northwind's written consent (see AIV-STD).
11. Liability for sub-processor failures as if they were the vendor's own.

## 3. International Transfers

Personal data may not be transferred or made accessible outside the jurisdiction in which it was collected without Legal approval. Transfers out of the EU, EEA, or UK require an approved transfer mechanism, normally the Standard Contractual Clauses or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, plus a transfer impact assessment completed by Privacy Counsel. Remote access from another country counts as a transfer.

## 4. Data Minimization

The Business Owner must justify each data category in the processing annex. Legal will strike categories that are not needed for the stated purpose. Sharing data beyond the annex is a breach of this standard and of the DPA.

## 5. Review and Amendment

The DPA is reviewed at each renewal (RNW-PRC) and whenever processing changes. A new data category, a new purpose, or a new processing location requires a DPA amendment before the change takes effect.

## 6. Roles

Privacy Counsel approves non-standard DPA language. The TPRM Office confirms a signed DPA is on file before onboarding approval (ONB-PRC Step 5). The Vendor Manager tracks sub-processor notices and forwards them to the Privacy Office within 2 business days of receipt.
