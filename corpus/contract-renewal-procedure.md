# Contract Renewal Procedure

Doc ID: RNW-PRC
Version: 1.6
Owner: Head of Procurement
Effective: 10 April 2026
Status: Current

## Section 1 — Purpose

A renewal is a fresh decision to continue a relationship, not an administrative formality. This procedure ensures the decision is taken with current risk and performance information and with enough time to exit or re-source if the answer is no.

## Section 2 — Renewal Notice Windows

The Vendor Manager must open a renewal case in ProcureNet no later than the following number of days before the contract expiry date:

| Tier | Renewal case opened |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 | 120 days before expiry |
| Tier 2 | 90 days before expiry |
| Tier 3 | 60 days before expiry |
| Tier 4 | 30 days before expiry |

ProcureNet sends reminders 30 days before each window opens. A renewal case opened late must be reported to the Head of Procurement, and a Tier 1 or Tier 2 case opened late is also reported to the TPRC.

## Section 3 — Renewal Risk Review

Before any renewal is approved, the Vendor Manager completes a renewal risk review covering:

1. Reassessment status. If the vendor's next reassessment under DUE-STD §5 falls due within 6 months of the renewal date, the reassessment is performed now as part of the renewal. An overdue reassessment blocks renewal.
2. Performance. The last four performance scorecards under PERF-STD, including any open performance improvement plan.
3. Open findings and exceptions. All open DUE-STD findings and EXC-PRC exceptions, with their expiry dates.
4. Incidents. Any incident notified under INCN-SLA during the term, and whether notification deadlines were met.
5. Scope change. Any change to data accessed, services supported, or spend that would change the tier under TIER-MTX.
6. Subcontractors. Any change to material subcontractors under FRTH-POL.
7. Concentration. For Tier 1 vendors, the TPRM Office confirms the renewal does not breach a threshold in CONC-STD.

## Section 4 — Re-Sourcing

A renewal that increases total contract value above $250,000, where the original award was below that amount, must be competitively re-sourced under PRC-POL unless a sole-source justification is approved.

## Section 5 — Auto-Renewal

5.1 Automatic renewal clauses are prohibited in Tier 1 and Tier 2 contracts. Every Tier 1 and Tier 2 renewal requires an affirmative decision recorded in ProcureNet.

5.2 Tier 3 and Tier 4 contracts may auto-renew only if the contract (a) limits each renewal term to 12 months or less and (b) allows Northwind to terminate for convenience on 30 days' written notice.

5.3 Where an inherited contract contains a non-compliant auto-renewal clause, Legal must issue a non-renewal notice before the clause's notice deadline and the contract must be renegotiated.

## Section 6 — Decision and Records

The Business Owner approves the renewal. Where the renewal risk review identifies a Critical or High finding without an approved exception, the renewal may not be approved. If the decision is not to renew, the Vendor Manager invokes the exit plan under EXIT-PLN at least 60 days before expiry for Tier 1 and Tier 2 vendors. Renewal records are retained with the contract under RET-SCH.
