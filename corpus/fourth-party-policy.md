# Fourth-Party (Subcontractor) Policy

Doc ID: FRTH-POL
Version: 1.4
Effective: 2025-11-01
Status: Current

## 1. Purpose

Our vendors' vendors are our risk too. When a vendor subcontracts part of a service, Northwind data and Northwind services may depend on a party with which Northwind has no contract. This policy defines how those fourth parties are disclosed, approved, and monitored.

## 2. Definitions

- **Fourth party** — any subcontractor, sub-processor, or supplier used by a Northwind vendor in delivering services to Northwind.
- **Material subcontractor** — a fourth party that (a) stores, processes, or can access Northwind Confidential or Restricted data or personal data; (b) performs a part of the service whose failure would disrupt a critical or important business service; or (c) hosts the vendor's production environment.

## 3. Disclosure

3.1 Tier 1 and Tier 2 vendors must disclose all material subcontractors during due diligence, including name, location, services performed, and data accessed.

3.2 Tier 1 vendors must give Northwind at least 60 days' prior written notice before adding or replacing a material subcontractor. Tier 2 vendors must give at least 30 days' notice, which aligns with the sub-processor notice period in DPA-REQ.

3.3 Northwind may object to a new material subcontractor. If the objection cannot be resolved, Northwind may terminate the affected services without penalty.

## 4. Approval

4.1 The TPRM Office assesses each new material subcontractor of a Tier 1 or Tier 2 vendor. The assessment covers security posture, location, sanctions screening (SANC-PRC), and concentration (CONC-STD).

4.2 Offshore subcontracting, meaning any material subcontractor performing services or accessing data outside the country in which the contracted vendor operates, requires approval from both the TPRM Office and Legal.

4.3 A new material subcontractor triggers an early reassessment of the vendor under TIER-MTX §3.

## 5. Flow-Down

The vendor contract must require the vendor to flow down to each material subcontractor obligations no less protective than its own for:

- confidentiality and data protection;
- information security;
- incident notification (INCN-SLA);
- business continuity (BCP-REQ);
- audit and regulator access (AUDT-GDE);
- data return and destruction at exit (EXIT-PLN).

The vendor remains fully liable for its subcontractors' performance and failures.

## 6. Inventory

The TPRM Office maintains a fourth-party inventory listing each material subcontractor and the vendors that depend on it. Tier 1 and Tier 2 vendors confirm their subcontractor list annually, and the inventory is updated within 10 business days of any notice under 3.2. The inventory feeds the quarterly concentration report under CONC-STD.

## 7. Incidents at a Fourth Party

An incident at a material subcontractor that affects Northwind data or services must be notified by the vendor as if it occurred at the vendor, under the deadlines in INCN-SLA. "Our subcontractor did not tell us" is not a defense under Northwind contracts.

## 8. Ownership

Questions on this policy go to the TPRM Office. Contract drafting questions go to Legal.
