# Procurement Policy

Doc ID: PRC-POL
Version: 5.1
Owner: Head of Procurement
Effective: 2025-12-01
Status: Current

## 1. Purpose

This policy ensures Northwind buys goods and services fairly, at a defensible price, and with the right approvals. It works alongside TPR-POL: procurement decides how we choose a vendor, TPRM decides whether the chosen vendor is safe to use.

## 2. Scope

All purchases of goods and services by any Northwind entity, regardless of funding source. Excluded: employee expense reimbursements, payroll, taxes, and payments to regulators.

## 3. Competitive Sourcing Thresholds

The threshold is based on the total contract value over the full initial term, including optional extensions.

| Total contract value | Minimum sourcing requirement |
|---|---|
| Under $25,000 | One written quote |
| $25,000 to $250,000 | Two written quotes |
| More than $250,000 | Three competitive bids through a formal RFP run by Procurement |

Splitting a requirement into smaller purchases to stay under a threshold is prohibited and is treated as a policy breach.

## 4. Sole-Source Justification

4.1 Where competitive sourcing is not possible, the Business Owner submits a sole-source justification in ProcureNet. Acceptable grounds are limited to: only one supplier can meet the requirement; continuation of an existing proprietary system where switching cost is prohibitive; a documented emergency; or a regulator-mandated provider.

4.2 Sole-source justifications are approved by the Head of Procurement. Where the total contract value exceeds $1,000,000, the CFO must also approve.

4.3 Cost or convenience alone is not an acceptable ground. "The team already likes this vendor" is not a justification.

## 5. Approval Authority

| Total contract value | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to $100,000 | Department head |
| $100,001 to $1,000,000 | Managing director of the business unit |
| More than $1,000,000 | CFO |

Approvals are recorded in ProcureNet. The approver must not be the requester.

## 6. No PO, No Pay

Every purchase over $5,000 requires an approved purchase order before the vendor begins work. Accounts Payable will not pay invoices that do not match an approved purchase order. A purchase order cannot be raised until the vendor is active under ONB-PRC.

## 7. Evaluation and Award

7.1 RFP evaluation criteria and weights are fixed before bids are opened. Risk criteria, including the vendor's likely tier under TIER-MTX, must carry at least 20% of the total weight for any RFP where the vendor will access Confidential or Restricted data.

7.2 Evaluators sign a conflict-of-interest declaration before reviewing bids (GIFT-POL). A conflicted evaluator must recuse.

7.3 Unsuccessful bidders are notified in writing within 10 business days of award.

## 8. Renewals and Changes

Renewals follow RNW-PRC. A change order that increases total contract value by more than 20% requires the same approval as a new purchase of the revised total value.

## 9. Breaches

Purchases made outside this policy are reported to the Head of Procurement and, where the vendor was not onboarded, to the TPRC as a TPR-POL breach.
