# Sanctions and Adverse Media Screening Procedure

Doc ID: SANC-PRC
Version: 3.1
Owner: Financial Crimes Compliance
Effective: 02/20/2026
Status: Current

## Section 1 — Purpose

Northwind may not pay, contract with, or provide services to a sanctioned person. This procedure defines how vendors and their owners are screened and how potential matches are resolved.

## Section 2 — Who Is Screened

- The vendor legal entity and any trading names.
- Beneficial owners holding 25% or more of the vendor. For Tier 1 vendors the threshold is 10% or more.
- Directors and senior officers named in the vendor's registration documents.
- Material subcontractors disclosed under FRTH-POL.

## Section 3 — Lists

Screening runs against, at minimum: the OFAC Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List and the OFAC consolidated non-SDN lists; the UN Security Council Consolidated List; the EU Consolidated Financial Sanctions List; and the UK HM Treasury Consolidated List.

## Section 4 — When Screening Occurs

4.1 At onboarding, before any diligence evidence is requested (ONB-PRC Step 3).

4.2 Continuously after onboarding. Every active vendor record is rescreened automatically every day against the current lists, regardless of tier.

4.3 Before any change to vendor bank details is applied.

4.4 When the contract is not executed within 90 days of diligence approval (DUE-STD §3.2).

## Section 5 — Adverse Media

Adverse media screening covers financial crime, fraud, bribery and corruption, sanctions evasion, human-rights abuse, and serious regulatory enforcement. It is performed at onboarding for Tier 1 and Tier 2 vendors and repeated annually for those tiers. Tier 3 and Tier 4 vendors receive sanctions screening only, unless a Business Owner or the TPRM Office requests adverse media screening.

## Section 6 — Match Handling

6.1 The screening tool generates alerts. Procurement Operations performs first-level review and closes clear false positives, recording the reason.

6.2 Any alert not closed as a false positive must be triaged by Financial Crimes Compliance (FCC) within 2 business days.

6.3 If FCC confirms a true match, it escalates to the Chief Compliance Officer and Legal within 24 hours. All payments to the vendor are blocked immediately, and the vendor master record is placed on hold.

6.4 Northwind staff must not tell the vendor why payments are held. Disclosure may breach sanctions or anti-tipping-off obligations; all communication goes through Legal.

6.5 A confirmed true match cannot be covered by an exception under EXC-PRC. The TPRM Office assesses invoking the stressed exit plan (EXIT-PLN §5).

## Section 7 — Adverse Media Findings

Material adverse media findings are referred to the TPRM Office and the Business Owner. The Business Owner must document a decision to proceed, proceed with conditions, or decline. Findings involving bribery or sanctions evasion also require FCC sign-off to proceed.

## Section 8 — Records

Screening results, alert dispositions, and FCC decisions are retained under RET-SCH. Records must show who closed each alert and why.
