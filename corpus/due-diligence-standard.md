# Vendor Due Diligence Standard

Doc ID: DUE-STD
Version: 3.0
Owner: TPRM Office
Effective: 2026-03-01
Status: Current

## 1. Purpose

This standard defines the evidence Northwind collects before contracting with a vendor and at each reassessment. It implements TPR-POL §4.2 and replaces the Vendor Due Diligence Standard v2.1 (DUE-OLD) in full.

## 2. Scope

All new vendors and all existing vendors at reassessment. The tier assigned under TIER-MTX determines which sections apply.

## 3. Timing

3.1 Due diligence must be completed and approved by the TPRM Office before contract signature. Procurement may not issue a purchase order against an unapproved vendor.

3.2 Diligence results are valid for 90 days. If the contract is not executed within 90 days of approval, the TPRM Office refreshes the sanctions screening and confirms no material change before signature.

## 4. Assessment Evidence

### 4.1 Security Questionnaire

Tier 1 and Tier 2 vendors complete the full Northwind Security Questionnaire (NSQ). Tier 3 vendors complete the NSQ Lite. The CISO office reviews responses and may request supporting evidence for any answer.

### 4.2 Independent Assurance Reports

Tier 1 and Tier 2 vendors must provide a SOC 2 Type II report. The report must cover a period of at least 6 months and must be dated no more than 12 months before the review date. SOC 2 Type I reports are not accepted as a substitute, because they test control design at a point in time and not operating effectiveness. An ISO/IEC 27001 certificate with its Statement of Applicability may substitute for a Tier 2 vendor only with written CISO office approval. Bridge letters are accepted to cover a gap of up to 3 months after the report period end.

### 4.3 Penetration Testing

Tier 1 vendors provide an executive summary of an independent penetration test performed within the last 12 months, including the status of any critical or high findings.

### 4.4 Other Evidence

Financial health is reviewed under FIN-STD, sanctions and adverse media under SANC-PRC, continuity under BCP-REQ, and cloud-specific controls under CLD-STD. AI service providers also complete the AIV-STD addendum.

## 5. Reassessment

Reassessment frequency follows TIER-MTX §3:

- Tier 1: every 12 months
- Tier 2: every 24 months
- Tier 3: every 36 months
- Tier 4: at renewal only

The Vendor Manager starts the reassessment no later than 60 days before the due date. A reassessment more than 30 days overdue is reported to the TPRC and blocks contract renewal (RNW-PRC).

## 6. Findings

6.1 The CISO office rates each finding Critical, High, Medium, or Low.

6.2 Critical and High findings must be remediated, or covered by an approved exception under EXC-PRC, before onboarding or renewal proceeds.

6.3 Medium findings require a vendor remediation plan with a target date no more than 180 days out. Low findings are recorded and tracked at the next reassessment.

## 7. Records

The completed due diligence file is retained under RET-SCH.
